01 — Features
Create With Confidence
Design tools that understand your brand. Every export, every template, every generation stays on-brand automatically.
12

12 Design Templates

Social posts, presentations, thumbnails, banners, stories, and more. Each template adapts to your brand kit automatically — colors, fonts, and spacing locked in before you start.

G

Agent Generation

Describe what you need and agents create it. Sapphira handles visuals, Calliope writes the copy, Thalia optimizes for engagement. Multi-agent collaboration produces finished assets, not rough drafts.

E

Project Export

Export to PNG, SVG, PDF, or directly to BackRoad for publishing. Every export includes metadata, version history, and RoadChain provenance. Your creative work is always traceable and yours.

B

Brand Kit

Define your colors, fonts, logos, and voice once. BlackBoard enforces consistency across every asset you create. Update the kit and every template refreshes. One source of truth for your brand.

02 — Creative Agents
Your Studio Crew
Four agents dedicated to the creative process. Each brings a distinct skill to your work.
CalliopeStoryteller
SapphiraVisual
ThaliaSocial
LyraPolish
C

Calliope — Storyteller

Writes headlines, body copy, taglines, and narrative arcs. Calliope understands your brand voice and adapts tone for every format — from tweet to whitepaper. She writes the words that make people stop scrolling.

S

Sapphira — Visual

Generates layouts, color compositions, and visual assets. Sapphira works from your brand kit to produce designs that feel cohesive. She handles the canvas so you can focus on the message.

T

Thalia — Social

Optimizes every asset for engagement. Thalia knows platform dimensions, trending formats, and audience patterns. She turns your creative work into content that performs — sized, cropped, and captioned for every channel.

L

Lyra — Polish

Refines and perfects. Lyra adjusts kerning, alignment, contrast, and flow. She catches what the eye misses — the spacing that feels off, the color that clashes, the line that could be tighter. Final pass before publish.
